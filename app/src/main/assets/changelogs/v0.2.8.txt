# Mjolnir v0.2.8 - Stability Update

First release of the maintained fork (github.com/riap0526/mjolnir).

## Stability
- Settings and gesture presets survive crashes and sudden power loss; a corrupted file is restored from its backup instead of resetting your setup.
- Top/bottom apps launch even if they are hidden in the blacklist or by "Show all apps", so a launch failure can no longer wipe your configuration.
- Home presses respond faster: no more scanning every installed app on each press.
- Fixed a preset named "custom" multiplying into dozens of untitled files.
- Fixed a 10-second period after a restart where Home presses were ignored.
- Lower background memory use: app icons load only while Mjolnir's screens are open.

## Home routing and gestures
- FOCUS: Home opens Home on the screen that has focus (thanks to ghgoodreau).
- Long press no longer triggers a second gesture when released.

## Diagnostics
- The diagnostics log now records why Mjolnir last stopped, settings recovery, real launch results and when the SafetyNet screen appears.
